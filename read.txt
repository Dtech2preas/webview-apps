# Pick n Pay Multichannel Retail Strategy (Section 5: Multiple Channels)

## Executive Summary  
Pick n Pay (PnP) is a **multi-format, multi-channel** retailer in South Africa【21†L179-L183】.  In groceries it operates supermarkets, hypermarkets, clothing and liquor stores (both company-owned and franchises) as well as a growing discount banner (Boxer)【21†L179-L187】.  PnP’s digital channels include its online shopping portal (scheduled delivery), the Pick n Pay *asap!* same-day app, and a partnership with Mr D (Takealot) to offer PnP Groceries via the Mr D app【21†L189-L193】【27†L74-L83】.  Customers can also collect online orders for free from participating stores【42†L148-L157】.  The Smart Shopper loyalty programme and eBucks rewards are integrated across channels【27†L79-L88】【45†L115-L123】, enabling unified pricing and promotions (e.g. PnP groceries on Mr D are sold at in-store prices)【27†L74-L83】.  This report reviews the definitions of multi/omni/cross-channel retailing, maps out PnP’s current channel mix and how they are integrated, illustrates customer journeys for different shopper personas, and evaluates PnP’s multichannel strategy with a SWOT analysis. It also compares PnP’s channel coverage against Woolworths and Checkers (Shoprite), identifies key omnichannel performance metrics, and offers recommendations and risk mitigations. 

## Defining Multi-, Omni- and Cross-Channel Retailing  
**Multichannel retailing** means selling through *multiple independent channels* (physical store, website, app, social, etc.), but with each channel largely operating its own inventory, pricing and promotions.  In a multichannel model each channel has its own strategy and customer experience【5†L139-L147】【8†L178-L186】. By contrast, **omnichannel retailing** *integrates* all channels into a seamless, unified experience – the focus is on the customer journey rather than the channel.  Data (customer profiles, loyalty points, cart contents) flows across channels so shoppers can start in one channel and continue in another without disruption【5†L179-L188】【8†L205-L213】.  For example, an omnichannel shopper might browse online, receive a mobile coupon, then pick up that order in-store, earning loyalty rewards transparently across all steps.  **Cross-channel** lies between these models: channels are connected (e.g. promotions link between email and website), but not fully seamless.  Customers can move between channels (e.g. see an email and then a related ad or shelf display) but the experience is still product- or promotion-focused rather than fully unified【8†L192-L200】.  

In practice, South African retailers now aim for an **omnichannel** model: they offer many channels *and* integrate them. For instance, Woolworths merged its on-demand *Dash* app into its main Woolies App, offering scheduled delivery, click-and-collect (free) and same-day delivery in one place【10†L91-L99】【48†L274-L283】. Pick n Pay similarly combines its online shop, *asap!* app and loyalty system so that customers enjoy consistent pricing and rewards across channels【21†L189-L193】【27†L74-L83】. 

## Pick n Pay’s Current Channel Mix  
PnP’s channel coverage is broad. Key channels include:  
- **Physical stores (brick-and-mortar)**: Over 2,200 stores across Pick n Pay, Pick n Pay Clothing, Pick n Pay Liquor, Boxer, Build, etc.【21†L179-L187】. Formats range from large hypermarkets to small convenience stores.  
- **E-commerce (scheduled online grocery)**: Pick n Pay’s traditional online shop (launched ~2000s) offers ~15,000+ products for delivery Mon–Sat (until 20:00)【42†L148-L157】. Customers can create shopping lists, view history and apply digital coupons.  
- **Same-day delivery (asap!)**: The *Pick n Pay asap!* app (combined with SmartShopper) provides on-demand grocery delivery in ~60 minutes for a flat fee (e.g. R35)【45†L88-L92】【45†L109-L113】. Over 30,000 items, including alcohol, are offered for rapid fulfilment, drawing from 400+ stores as of 2026【45†L84-L92】【52†L176-L179】. This was built by integrating the Bottles/Supermart startup (now fully owned by PnP) with PnP’s inventory and using third-party delivery drivers【52†L137-L145】.  
- **Click & Collect**: Customers can shop online and pick up orders (often free of charge) at a store of their choice【42†L148-L157】. Pick n Pay personal shoppers pick and pack items by reservation time.  
- **Partner platforms**: In 2022 PnP launched *PnP Groceries on Mr D*, allowing customers in major metros (Cape Town, Joburg, expanding) to order PnP groceries via the Mr D (Takealot) app【19†L47-L56】【27†L74-L83】. This offers “10,000+” PnP items at in-store prices, delivered in an hour【19†L61-L69】【27†L74-L83】. Smart Shopper points and FNB eBucks apply to these orders【19†L69-L73】【27†L79-L88】.  
- **Mobile app + loyalty**: Pick n Pay’s unified *asap!+SmartShopper* mobile app (launched by 2026) lets customers shop the full range and use Smart Shopper loyalty. It boasts 30,000+ products, in-store pricing, and the ability to earn/spend Smart Shopper points and eBucks on all purchases【45†L84-L92】【45†L115-L123】. The app integrates features like AI search, order tracking, and PnP “Clubs” (wine club, baby club etc.) for targeted deals【45†L88-L92】【45†L137-L145】.  
- **Loyalty & payments**: The Smart Shopper programme (with FNB partnership) unifies loyalty across channels. Customers earn points and eBucks in-store, on the website, in the app and via Mr D【19†L69-L73】【45†L115-L123】. Smart Shopper vouchers and targeted discounts apply both online and offline.  
- **Social media / digital marketing**: PnP uses Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, email and SMS to engage customers (not unique channels for shopping but important touchpoints).    
- **Wholesale / B2B**: PnP does not have a major B2B division; its focus is retail. (By contrast Shoprite has formal B2B under Score, but PnP’s involvement in supply to smaller businesses is via franchises and its “Boost Your Biz” SME programme【35†L0-L3】.)  

In summary, Pick n Pay covers all major customer channels: multiple store formats, full e-commerce with delivery and pickup, a dedicated same-day app, an integrated mobile app, and a modern loyalty programme. Table 1 (below) compares these channels with those of Woolworths and Checkers.

## Channel Integration (Systems and Processes)  
PnP has invested in a centralised IT backbone to connect channels. All channels feed into a single SAP platform for inventory planning and replenishment【21†L273-L277】.  This ensures consistent stock data: online and store inventories update nightly, so the website knows if an item is in stock.  Pricing and promotions are also unified – e.g. prices on Pick n Pay’s Mr D listings match in-store prices【27†L74-L83】. The PnP app and website allow customers to log in with a single account, and Smart Shopper loyalty is integrated: customers earn the same points and see the same coupons whether shopping in-store, on the web, in the app or via Mr D【27†L79-L88】【45†L115-L123】.  In short, **inventory, pricing, promotions and loyalty are largely aligned across channels**. This does require operational coordination: in-store personal shoppers are trained to pick for Click & Collect and asap! orders, and PnP’s logistics (own and Mr D drivers) must efficiently fulfil deliveries.  

For example, the Pick n Pay–Mr D model works as follows【27†L74-L83】: a customer orders on the Mr D app (with PnP products), chooses delivery; the order is sent to the designated Pick n Pay store’s picking system; staff pick the items; a Mr D contracted driver then collects and delivers. Through the linking of systems, the customer sees live stock availability and can earn loyalty points on that sale【27†L79-L88】. Likewise, Click & Collect orders are sent to the closest store, where items are held until pick-up time. The end-to-end process aims to be seamless (as advertised: “the world of Pick n Pay in the palm of your hand”【45†L88-L92】), though execution can be complex. 

## Customer Journey Examples  
To illustrate multichannel usage, consider three shopper personas:

- **In-Store Shopper**: This customer prefers shopping in a physical PnP store. They enter a Pick n Pay branch, browse aisles, and may use the mobile app to scan barcodes or check product info on the spot (e.g. looking up a recipe or loyalty offer). They add items to a trolley. At checkout, they present their Smart Shopper card or log in on the checkout kiosk to apply coupons【42†L148-L157】. After paying, they earn loyalty points. This journey is channel-centric: the shopper starts and ends in-store, but may have had digital interactions (app deals, social ads) influencing the visit.

- **Online Shopper**: This customer does nearly all shopping via digital channels. They open the PnP website or mobile app, log into their Smart Shopper account, and add products to an online cart. They choose between scheduled home delivery or Click & Collect. If delivery, they select a date and pay (earning loyalty points on checkout)【42†L148-L157】【27†L79-L88】. The order is then picked at a store and delivered, or held for pickup. Throughout, the shopper enjoys one account and consistent offers (e.g. they see the same sale prices and Smart Shopper coupons as in-store). Their experience is seamless: past purchases and saved lists are visible on any device, and delivery trackers update in real time.

- **Hybrid Shopper**: A customer who mixes channels. For example, they research groceries on the PnP app at home (adding some staples to a cart). On the way to the store, they might adjust that online order (e.g. adding perishable that they’d prefer fresh). They opt for Click & Collect: the next day they pick up the pre-packed order and then continue shopping inside the store for additional items. At checkout they pay for the in-store items, with Smart Shopper points applied to the entire combined purchase. Alternatively, a hybrid shopper might start in-store, then scan items with the app to add to an online cart for later home delivery of heavy goods. In each case, the transitions are designed to be smooth: the customer is recognized (via app or loyalty), and their activity on one channel is visible on another.

## SWOT Analysis for Pick n Pay’s Multichannel Strategy  

- **Strengths:**  
  - *Extensive channel portfolio:* PnP has broad coverage (stores, web, app, delivery) with an integrated loyalty scheme【21†L179-L187】【45†L115-L123】. This gives customers choice and convenience (e.g. click-and-collect, same-day, etc.) and deepens customer engagement.  
  - *Brand and loyalty:* Pick n Pay is a well-known, trusted brand in
